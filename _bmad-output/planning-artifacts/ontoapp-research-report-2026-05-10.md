# Research Report: Ontoapp

**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Author:** Mary / BMad Analyst
**Research Type:** Mixed domain, market and technical research

---

## Research Overview

This research examines the Ontoapp concept from three angles: domain relevance, market differentiation, and technical feasibility. The goal is to validate the app's positioning as an ontology-driven reflective tool and identify the most promising MVP path.

## Research Goals

- Validate the Ontoapp value proposition
- Identify adjacent markets and competitors
- Clarify technical risks and core architecture needs
- Recommend the highest-priority MVP scope

## Context

Ontoapp is described as a tool for ontological education: a mobile app that helps users practice presence, self-reflection, and meaning-making through a Body/Mind/Purpose triad. Key ideas already generated include:

- confrontation questions that expose inauthenticity
- anti-gamification mechanics
- ritualized presence instead of habit formation
- dynamic balancing of reflection focus
- a symbolic visualization called the "Forma do Ser"

## Domain Analysis

### Core domain

- **Ontology and existential reflection**: Ontoapp is positioned in the domain of self-discovery and reflective practice, distinct from ordinary wellness and productivity apps.
- **Three-pillar framework**: Body, Mind, Purpose is a strong domain model that supports a differentiated educational narrative.
- **Anti-gamification**: The app’s core claim is the rejection of conventional reward mechanics, making it a domain-specific product for users seeking deeper reflection rather than quick fixes.

### User needs

Potential user archetypes:

- seekers dissatisfied with superficial mindfulness apps
- philosophical learners who want practice, not just content
- people experiencing autopilot living and craving deeper presence
- users who value privacy, silence, and non-judgmental reflection

Key domain needs:

- safe confrontation with self-deception
- structured rituals for presence
- tools to notice and shift patterns of avoidance
- meaningful feedback that is not performance-based

## Market Analysis

### Adjacent markets

- **Mindfulness and meditation apps**: Calm, Headspace, Waking Up
- **Journaling/self-reflection apps**: Day One, Journey, Reflectly
- **Habit-building and productivity tools**: Habitica, Streaks, Momentum
- **Therapeutic journaling / mental health**: Moodnotes, Replika, Sanvello

### Differentiation

Ontoapp can stand apart by:

- focusing on ontological presence rather than relaxation or productivity
- explicitly rejecting streaks, points, and gamified engagement
- using philosophical language and existential prompts
- offering “interruptions” and silence as features, not just daily tasks
- modelling balance through the Body/Mind/Purpose triad and the Forma do Ser

### Competitive opportunities

- There is a gap for an app that treats reflection as a lived practice instead of a checklist.
- Most existing apps are either relaxation-oriented or habit-oriented; few explicitly address the experience of being inauthentic.
- The combination of audio presence rituals + dynamic focus shifting is a compelling differentiator.

## Technical Research

### Core technical components

1. **Ritual audio capture**
   - short morning audio recording
   - analysis of tone, pauses, and content
   - generation of a tailored confrontation prompt

2. **Trigger detection engine**
   - behavioral signals for digital autopilot / escape
   - light usage pattern detection, ideally privacy-preserving
   - asynchronous interruption mechanism

3. **Dynamic focus system**
   - the "Centro de Gravidade" engine that balances Body/Mind/Purpose
   - uses input frequency and content themes to shift recommendations

4. **Visualization system**
   - the "Forma do Ser" radar/triad visualization
   - qualitative presentation of balance and imbalance

5. **Privacy-aware data architecture**
   - persistent vs ephemeral input handling
   - optional ghost-mode inputs that are processed but not stored
   - clear consent for any usage or behavioral detection

### Feasibility and risks

- **Feasible MVP**: audio capture + simple prompt generation + nightly text reflection + symbolic visualization.
- **Medium risk**: reliable detection of "fuga" behavior without invasive tracking.
- **High risk**: building a meaningful tone-analysis engine that feels accurate and not intrusive.
- **Data risk**: users must trust the app with sensitive inner experience, so privacy and transparency are critical.

## Recommendation

### MVP focus

Prioritize the following core capabilities:

1. **Embodied Reflection** (MORPHO #1) — morning audio ritual
2. **Existential Interruption** (MORPHO #2) — a first-generation escape/fugue trigger, even if initially manual or heuristic-based
3. **Internal Dialogue Capture** (MORPHO #3) — nightly text reflection
4. **Centro de Gravidade** — basic balance logic that surfaces which pillar needs attention
5. **Forma do Ser** — qualitative visualization of the triad state

### Suggested next actions

- define target user persona(s) and use cases explicitly
- run a lightweight competitor gap analysis for "reflection vs productivity" apps
- prototype the audio ritual and the Form of Ser visualization first
- plan a privacy-first MVP architecture with explicit ephemeral-mode handling

## Summary

Ontoapp has a strong conceptual position in a niche that values reflection over productivity. The primary value proposition is not doing more, but noticing more. The most defensible MVP path is a minimalist, ritualized experience with strong privacy and a distinctive anti-gamification stance.
