# Ontoapp: A Reflection App for Authentic Presence

## A reflective digital companion for people who want to stop living on autopilot and start noticing their body, mind and purpose.

**São Paulo, 10 May 2026** — Ontoapp is a mobile reflection tool that helps users move from automatic doing to intentional being. It is built for people who are tired of shallow wellness apps and want a privacy-first space for presence, existential confrontation and meaning-making.

Most apps today either chase productivity, relaxation, or habit streaks. They rarely help users see that the real problem is not a behavior gap — it is a lived experience gap. People are coping with autopilot through scrolling, surface journaling, or checkbox routines, and they lose touch with what they feel, what they think, and why they act.

Ontoapp changes that by creating a pattern of presence instead of a pattern of performance. Users begin with a short embodied ritual, reflect on their internal dialogue, and receive gentle interruptions when they slip into digital escape. The app also makes balance visible with a symbolic Body/Mind/Purpose model called the Forma do Ser.

> "Our goal is not to make users more productive. It is to help them notice when they are living a life that they did not choose."
> — Product Team, Ontoapp

### How It Works

1. The user opens Ontoapp in the morning and is guided through a brief embodied audio ritual. The app listens for tone and presence, then offers a tailored reflection prompt.
2. Throughout the day, Ontoapp watches for patterns of autopilot behavior and, when appropriate, offers a gentle interruption asking the user what they are avoiding.
3. At night, the user writes an internal dialogue capture that surfaces the tension between self-justification and authenticity.
4. The app tracks these interactions without gamification and uses the Centro de Gravidade to identify whether the user needs more focus on Body, Mind, or Purpose.
5. The Forma do Ser visualization shows the user how integrated or imbalanced their current state is, making inner patterns visible without reward mechanics.

> "I used to feel like I was just going through the motions. Ontoapp helped me notice how much of my day was autopilot, and it gave me a way to stop and ask what I was actually avoiding."
> — Early user persona, reflective seeker

### Getting Started

- Download Ontoapp and complete the onboarding consent flow.
- Start with the morning embodied presence ritual on a day when you have 5 minutes of quiet.
- Use the app’s prompts at the moment it detects distraction, or when you sense you are scrolling to avoid something.
- At night, write one honest internal dialogue entry.
- Review the Forma do Ser once per week to see whether your balance is leaning toward Body, Mind, or Purpose.

## Customer FAQ

### Q: How is Ontoapp different from meditation or journaling apps?

A: Ontoapp is not primarily about relaxation or productivity. It is about noticing inauthentic living and giving the user tools to confront it. Meditation apps teach calm. Journaling apps collect entries. Ontoapp deliberately surfaces the user’s avoidance, shifts focus between body, mind, and purpose, and validates silence as a meaningful response.

### Q: Will Ontoapp track everything I do on my phone?

A: No. Ontoapp uses only lightweight, privacy-first heuristics to identify moments of digital autopilot. It is designed to minimize intrusive tracking, and users can opt out of any behavioral detection. The core product works with morning audio, nightly reflection, and optional interruption prompts.

### Q: What if I don't want my reflection to be stored?

A: Ontoapp supports ephemeral modes for sensitive honesty. Some inputs can be processed to generate prompts but not stored permanently. This gives users a safer place to express what they really feel without creating a permanent archive of every private moment.

### Q: Can I use Ontoapp if I already meditate or journal?

A: Yes. Ontoapp is designed to complement those practices by focusing on the gaps they often miss: autopilot behavior, inauthentic decision-making, and the sense of living disconnected from purpose. It can sit alongside meditation and journaling as a tool for deeper presence.

## Internal FAQ

### Q: What is the most important success metric for the first release?

A: The primary success metric is whether users report an increased ability to notice autopilot living and make at least one intentional corrective choice per week. Engagement should be driven by insight, not by reward loops.

### Q: Which feature is most critical for MVP?

A: The morning embodied ritual is the most critical feature because it establishes the app’s unique experience and differentiates it from other wellness tools. Without it, Ontoapp risks feeling like another journaling or reminders app.

### Q: What risks should we watch in the next stage?

A: Key risks are overpromising AI tone analysis, creating a tracking experience that feels invasive, and accidentally slipping into gamified design patterns. We should prioritize clarity, privacy, and the anti-gamification promise.

## The Verdict

Ontoapp has a strong conceptual position as a non-gamified, ontological reflection tool. Its core strength is the combination of presence rituals, refusal of reward mechanics, and a balance model that speaks to a deeper quality of lived experience.

What needs more validation:
- whether users will adopt a morning ritual without explicit productivity benefits
- how much behavior detection can be delivered without feeling intrusive
- whether the Forma do Ser is intuitive enough to communicate inner balance without metrics

Overall, the product is promising if it stays true to the idea that reflection should feel like a mirror, not a game.
