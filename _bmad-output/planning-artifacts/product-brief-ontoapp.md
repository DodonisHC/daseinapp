# Product Brief: Ontoapp

## Executive Summary

Ontoapp is a reflective mobile experience designed to help people move from autopilot existence to authentic presence. Instead of reward loops, habit streaks, or productivity metrics, Ontoapp guides users through curated rituals, existential provocations, and a dynamic balance model across Body, Mind, and Purpose.

The app is built for users who are tired of surface-level wellness tools and want a practical space to notice their presence, confront avoidance, and reconnect with meaning. Ontoapp delivers this through a morning embodied ritual, a quiet internal dialogue practice, a behavior-aware interruption system, and a symbolic visualization called the Forma do Ser.

## The Problem

Many people feel disconnected from their daily experience. They move through routines without noticing their body, repeat stories in their minds, and postpone decisions that matter. Existing wellness apps often focus on relaxation, productivity, or habit formation, but they rarely address the core issue: the user is living inauthentically and is unaware of it.

The consequences are real: wasted time, emotional fatigue, decision avoidance, and a sense that life is being lived for external stimuli rather than internal purpose. Users cope by scrolling, tracking accomplishments, or checking in superficially — none of which creates sustained inner alignment.

## The Solution

Ontoapp creates a reflective architecture for intentional being:

- **Morning Embodied Ritual:** A short guided audio ritual that brings bodily presence into the start of the day.
- **Internal Dialogue Capture:** A nightly writing practice that surfaces the tension between justification and honesty.
- **Existential Interruption:** A gentle, presence-oriented intervention when the app detects digital escape or autopilot behavior.
- **Dynamic Balance Engine:** The Centro de Gravidade adjusts the app’s focus across Body, Mind, and Purpose based on the user’s input patterns.
- **Forma do Ser Visualization:** A qualitative radar-like visualization that shows how integrated or imbalanced the user’s current state is.

This is not a habits app. It is a reflection space that tracks patterns of presence and avoidance, then encourages users toward more authentic choices.

## What Makes This Different

- **Anti-gamification by design:** no streaks, points, or badges. Progress is measured through presence and balance, not game mechanics.
- **Ontological framing:** the product speaks to existential authenticity instead of stress reduction or productivity gains.
- **Dynamic adjustment:** the app does not force a single flow; it senses which pillar is neglected and shifts focus accordingly.
- **Privacy-first reflection:** some inputs are intentionally ephemeral, giving users a safe place to express honest inner experience without permanent storage.
- **Silence as a feature:** the app validates non-input and quietness — the absence of data is itself meaningful.

## Who This Serves

Primary users:

- Individuals who already practice mindfulness or journaling but feel those tools are too shallow.
- People experiencing autopilot living and wanting a more intentional life.
- Reflective learners who value philosophy, presence, and meaning more than optimization.

Secondary users:

- Early adopters of mental wellness tools seeking alternative experiences.
- Coaches or facilitators who want to recommend a privacy-sensitive, non-gamified reflection app.

## Success Criteria

Ontoapp is successful when users:

- complete the morning embodied ritual at least twice a week
- report feeling more connected to bodily presence and less on autopilot
- recognize and name avoidance patterns through the Existential Interruption feature
- use the Forma do Ser to understand their balance across Body, Mind, and Purpose
- choose intentional, purpose-aligned actions rather than only tracking habits

Business success signals:

- onboarding completion rate of 60% or higher for the ritual experience
- repeat engagement driven by deliberate reflection, not addictive loops
- positive qualitative feedback on privacy and non-judgmental design

## Scope

Included in the first release:

- morning audio ritual with guidance and audio analysis for prompt generation
- nightly text-based internal dialogue capture
- a lightweight behavior detection trigger for escape/interruption
- a basic Centro de Gravidade engine to shift focus across the three pillars
- the Forma do Ser qualitative visualization
- privacy and optional ephemeral input handling

Out of scope for MVP:

- full wearable integration
- complex AI tone analysis beyond simple audio-state detection
- large-scale social or sharing features
- gamified achievement systems

## Vision

If Ontoapp succeeds, it becomes the trusted companion for people who want to live with deeper presence rather than simply feel better. In 2-3 years, Ontoapp can expand into:

- richer experiential pathways for different life transitions
- evidence-based ontological learning journeys
- a library of personalized reflection rituals
- a trusted “mirror app” for coaches, therapists, and reflective communities

The long-term product is less about daily tasks and more about helping users recognize when they are living from the edge of their own awareness — then choosing to come back.
